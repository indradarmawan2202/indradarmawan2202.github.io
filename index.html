( 00 )  Title trackWhy signal / noise?

Signal

/ˈsɪɡ.nəl/  noun
  1. The pattern worth keeping: the relevant passage in a retrieval pipeline, the true label hiding under noisy annotations, the metric that actually moved.
  2. What I try to ship: features you can measure.

Noise

/nɔɪz/  noun
  1. Everything that looks like signal but isn't: label noise, hallucinated answers, vanity metrics.
  2. Also: Deftones at full volume while the model trains.

My job is separating the two. My playlist is mostly the second one.
